# Demo — firm roster

## Problem

An owner who invites someone into their firm has no way to see who ended up with access.

## Actors

- **Firm owner**: wants to see, at a glance, exactly who can reach their firm's data.
- **Auditor**: wants to export the roster.

## What changes

A firm gets a roster.

## Non-goals

- **Changing a role from the roster.** Different risk.

## Deliberate unknowns

- **Paging.** Cheap if wrong.
